If this is your first time with us, no worries! Just follow the directions below and you're good to go!

  1. Choose a service – Select a barangay service you need (e.g., clearance, indigency certificate, business permit).
  2. Pick a date – Choose your preferred appointment date from the available slots.
  3. Specify details (if Others) – If your service falls under "Others," type in a short description of what you need.
  4. Confirm and submit – Review your selections and tap Book Appointment to get your queue number.
