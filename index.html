About Laan Bayan

Mission & Purpose

Laan Bayan is a barangay appointment and service availability system built to cut down on wasted trips and long walk-in lines. Residents can check what services are open, book a time slot, and track their place in the queue from anywhere — no more guessing whether the office is even open today.

What we're solving

Long unplanned queues at the barangay hall, unclear service hours, and no way to know ahead of time whether a request can even be processed that day. Laan Bayan gives every service a live status — available, full, or closed — so residents only show up when it counts.

Our commitment

Laan Bayan is maintained by the barangay office and is continuously updated based on resident feedback. The goal stays the same: less time waiting in line, more time back in your day.
